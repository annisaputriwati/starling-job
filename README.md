**The Starling Job** — a top-down stealth heist game. Play as Wren, a professional thief infiltrating the Aurelia Museum at night to steal the Starling Diamond. Read the guards' patrols and vision cones, slip past sweeping security cameras, toss decoy coins, EMP the cameras, pick the locks, grab the loot — and reach the getaway van before the alarm catches you. Three nights of rising difficulty: the Grand Hall, the Security Wing, and the Vault.

An entry in the [Make the best heist game](https://make.fun/bounty/best-heist-game-20261122-0559) bounty on make.fun.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
# (or simply open game/index.html — it plays fully offline)
```

Controls: WASD / arrows move · Shift sneak · hold E to pick locks and grab loot · click to toss a decoy coin · Q fires an EMP · M mute · P pause.
